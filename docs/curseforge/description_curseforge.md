**Golem Garage** lets you build your own golems out of parts and put them to work.

![Build your golems](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PCPx00/golem-garage/main/docs/curseforge/banner_build.gif)

Every golem has five parts: a head, torso, arms, legs and a core. Each part is a shape made from a material, and you can see it on the golem: iron arms look like iron, gold legs look like gold. The shape decides what a part does (wheels, spider legs, scythes, claws, lanterns, furnaces) and the material decides how strong and fast it is.

![Give them jobs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PCPx00/golem-garage/main/docs/curseforge/banner_jobs.gif)

Teach a golem a job with simple rules like "when its inventory is full, put items in the chest". Golems can farm, haul items between chests, guard an area, follow you, and pass items to each other.

![The Garage](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PCPx00/golem-garage/main/docs/curseforge/banner_garage.gif)

Golems don't die. They wear down and break down, and you repair them in a Garage you build yourself, where lasers fix them while you watch.

![Cubs and outfits](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PCPx00/golem-garage/main/docs/curseforge/banner_cubs.gif)

Cubs are tiny golem friends that follow you and carry your things. Dress them up in craftable outfits, from cool to goth to cute.

A craftable in-game manual explains everything.

*Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge. Works with JEI and Jade.*
