# Golem Garage: build status

Mod id `golemgarage`, version 0.12.0, Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.264. By xTrip.
The player guide is `docs/GUIDE.md` (generated by `python3 tools/golemgen/gen.py`).

## What 0.12.0 changed

- client/GarageSkin: formed-garage overlay. Every visible face of the room's shell (one block out from the bay)
  gets a panel from textures/entity/garage_skin.png (tools/golemgen/skin.py), 0.004 off the face. Face list cached
  per controller, rebuilt every 20 ticks with lighting. Panels fly in by distance from the controller on forming.
  Props: hoist beam, workbench, tyres, drums, jerry cans, loose chains, roof spikes (pointed dripstone), exhaust
  stack (smoke from the controller's client tick). Controller, emitters, doors and see-through blocks are skipped.
- Tested in the garage test world: transforms in about 2 s with sparks; breaking a wall block reverts it; the
  repair at night inside the transformed room looks like the reference.

## What 0.11.0 changed

- Repair visuals restyled after a reference image: jagged forking bolts in bursts (GarageControllerBlockEntity.firePower,
  34-tick cycle staggered per emitter, shared by client particles and server sounds), impact flashes, sparks, embers and
  smoke, golem shake, hoist chains, hanging lanterns. The scanning ring is gone.
- Tested at night in the garage test world: bolts, chains, lanterns and particles all visible; screenshots and a GIF.

## What 0.10.0 changed

- Garage: formed state checked every 2 s by the controller and synced (bay bounds, lit emitters). Renderer draws
  the neon sign, inside status panel, floor lines, hazard ring, idle lift pad and spinning warning lights.
- New blocks: Garage Door (roller door with block entity and renderer, group open/close, auto-open for golems and
  cubs, redstone, right-click hold) and Garage Window. Emitters have a LIT state.
- garage_walls tag: doors, windows, glass blocks and panes, concrete, smooth stone, polished andesite, iron blocks.
  Glass (any wall-tag block) allowed in the ceiling.
- Cubs: per-cub slot around the owner, idle wander, pushable (but never push players), catch-up teleport to a
  free spot.

## Tests run for 0.10.0

- Client test world (tools/testworld/live_client.sh garage): built a 5x5 Garage with door and windows. The sign
  showed OPEN, then REPAIRING 36% during a repair; the golem and beams were visible through the windows; the
  inside panel showed READY; the door stayed shut, rolled up when a golem came near and closed after.
- Eight cubs followed the player: all at different spots, closest pair 1.1 blocks apart, all within 3.4 blocks.

## What 0.9.0 changed
- **Modes** (`GolemMode`): Rules (default), Aggressive (follows; attacks hostiles within 12 blocks in sight),
  Peaceful (follows; never attacks, even when hit), Guard (follows; fights only mobs that attack its owner).
  Any mode but Rules overrides the rules. Set from the Status tab button, `/golemgarage mode`, synced and
  saved (also in golem items). Shown in Jade, the Rules tab (paused banner) and `/golemgarage debug`.
- **Sounds:** regenerated softer (low-passed thuds, sine beeps, quieter peaks), lower play volumes, golem
  sounds attenuate at 12 blocks, ambient every 45 s instead of 30 s. `SoundCrowd` gives each kind of sound
  (steps, work, ambient, fighting) a nearby budget: each extra nearby golem that just made the same kind of
  sound makes it quieter, and past a few it's skipped. New client setting `golemVolume`.

## Tests run for 0.9.0
- Dedicated server with a player: an Aggressive golem killed every husk that came within range; Peaceful and
  Guard golems hit by a husk didn't fight back; a husk attacking the owner was killed by the Guard golem.
- 20 golems strolling together: about 60 sounds per 10 seconds played (many at reduced volume), the rest skipped.
- Client: the Mode button cycles Guard to Rules and the server follows; the Rules tab shows the paused banner
  while a mode is on.

## What 0.8.0 changed
- **Commands:** `/golemgarage` (or `/gg`). Players: list, find, recall (works on unloaded golems and across
  dimensions), rename, stop, resume. Operators: give (golems by material and preset, cubs by color), repair,
  owner, count, purge (also removes unloaded golems when they next load), debug. A server-wide golem registry
  (saved with the world) remembers every golem's owner, name and last position.
- **Settings:** max golems per chunk, work range from owner, playerDamage (NEVER / PVP / ALWAYS, never the
  owner), killsCountAsOwner (XP and player-only loot), dropped golem/cub item despawn time, disabled jobs,
  modules and part shapes, blocked materials, per-material stat multipliers (health, durability, speed, power,
  work, damage), cub magnet range, max cubs per player, player commands on/off and recall cooldown.
- **Golem Dock:** a craftable charging pad; Linker Home mode; new "rest at its dock" job (slow recharge and
  heal; the pad lights up while a golem is docked).
- **Groups:** Linker Group mode with shift + scroll to pick 1-9; right-click golems to add them; "Copy to group"
  in the Rules tab shares rules with the whole group. Group shows in the panel, Jade and /golemgarage list.
- **Modpack support:** datapack golem materials (`data/<pack>/golem_material/<id>.json`: item tag, tier,
  borrowed look, tint, multipliers, traits), synced to players, craftable with every part shape through one
  recipe per shape, tinted on golems and item icons. Example pack in `docs/examples/mythril_pack`. Item tags
  for parts (and per slot), modules, outfits (and per style), golems, cubs, Garage blocks and tools.
- **Faster farms:** crop, storage and redstone-block scans are cached (a full scan at most every 2 seconds per
  golem, with cached hits re-checked) and storage lookups walk only nearby block entities. With 60 golems
  waiting on crops, server tick time dropped from about 5.2 ms to 4.3 ms in testing.
- Manual: Commands, For Modpacks, Groups and Golem Dock entries, and every new setting.

## Tests run for 0.8.0

- Behavior run: dock resting (pad lit, power refilled), a disabled job reporting "turned off", plus the earlier

- Client: Linker Group mode scroll, group in Jade and the panel, "Copy to group" (copied to the other golem).

## What 0.7.0 changed
- **Renamed to Golem Garage** (mod id `golemgarage`, was `modulargolems`). Items and golems from 0.6.0 worlds
  don't carry over.
- **Cub outfits:** 24 craftable pieces in four styles (cool, goth, cute, fancy) across head, body and accessory
  slots. Right-click a cub with a piece or use its new wardrobe screen; a matching set of three gets its own
  sparkles.
- **Upgrade modules:** Module Casing plus seven modules (Magnet, Lamp, Speed, Storage, Plating, Regeneration,
  Self-Repair), two sockets per golem, removable from the Status tab.
- **Work areas and teamwork:** Linker area corners, "outside its area" trigger, "patrol its area" and "give to a
  golem" actions; areas limit strolling, fighting, pickup and harvesting. Linker overlay shows links and areas.
- **Any storage:** chest actions and the Garage's payment use item-handler storage, so modded storage works.
- **Golem paint** with dyes (water bucket washes it off).
- **Animations:** idle fidgets, a work reach, and a 1.5 second slump before a breakdown.
- **Floating status icons** for stopped, stuck, broken part, no power and low power.
- **Sounds:** 17 sound events with subtitles (footsteps by leg type, work, hurt, attack, breakdown, Garage, cubs).
- **Advancements:** a 19-goal Golem Garage tab, driven by a custom `golemgarage:golem_event` criterion.
- **Config:** server and client settings files (repair time and cost, wear, weather, copper, power drain, golem cap,
  other players' access, harvesting and mobGriefing, the free manual, the Garage plan, status icons).
- **Multiplayer:** golems sync their owner; only the owner (or an operator, or anyone if the server allows it) can
  open, edit, link, paint or pick them up. Tested with a dedicated server and two players.
- **JEI** (subtypes, info pages, Garage Repair recipes) and **Jade** (golem power, job and worst part; cub outfit),
  both optional.
- **Translations:** every screen, tooltip, chat message, status line and rule phrase is in the language file
  (statuses travel as keys, so each player sees them in their own language). The manual loads
  `manual/<language>/book.json` when a translation exists.
- Manual: 14 chapters and 119 entries (new: Upgrade Modules, Settings and Mods, Work Areas, Teamwork, Any Storage,
  Paint, Outfits).

## What 0.6.0 changed
- **Friendlier Rules tab:** each rule reads as a sentence ("When its power is below 20% -> recharge at a
  redstone block, unless ..."). Click any colored part to pick from a grouped list with a description of each
  choice; numbers change with a click, right-click or the scroll wheel. Rules can be moved up or down and
  deleted, a new rule is added in two guided steps, Quick jobs load presets, and Undo reverts the last change.
- **Pick-up hotkey:** G (rebindable under Controls > Golem Garage) picks up the golem or cub you're looking
  at, up to 6 blocks away. Picking up (hotkey or shift + right-click) now only works on your own golems and
  cubs, or unowned ones.
- **New item art:** every part, golem and cub icon is now a 32x32 render of the real 3D model in its material,
  lit and outlined, with glowing eyes and cores. Steel ingot, repair kit, blueprint, Golem Linker and the
  manual are redrawn at 32x32.
- **Garage build plan:** holding a Garage Controller shows a see-through plan of the room (walls, floor,
  ceiling, emitters, controller, and anything in the way in red) with a checklist of what's left. Before
  placing it previews where the room would go; after, it follows the placed controller. Sneak + scroll picks
  3x3, 5x5, 7x7 or 9x9. The controller now remembers which way it was placed.

## What 0.5.0 changed
- **Golem Engineer's Manual:** a craftable guide book (book + iron ingot), given once to every player on first
  join. 12 chapters and 103 entries: getting started, anatomy and formulas, every part shape (golem preview,
  numbers in every material, abilities, recipe), every material (matched-set stats, traits, all its parts),
  wear and repair, the Garage (with diagrams), power, rules with every trigger and action, all six presets with
  setup steps, tools, Cubs, optional mods, and messages and fixes. Pages show live 3D golems, crafting grids
  read from the game's recipes, clickable item links with tooltips, and a search box. Content is generated from
  the same data as the game (`tools/golemgen/manual.py`), so its numbers always match.

## What 0.4.0 changed
- **Parts look like what they're made of.** Every part is a shape plus a material (22 shapes x 37 materials).
  Iron arms render as iron arms, gold legs as gold legs, and so on, on the golem itself, on the part items,
  and in the golem's panel. Each material has its own hand-painted palette and finish (ore veins, gem sparkle,
  copper patina, glowing veins on uranium and vibranium).
- **Shapes change the body.** Spider legs, wheels, treads, scythes, claws, hammer arms, lantern and visor heads,
  cargo barrels, furnace chests and five cores all have their own models and animations.
- **Per-part wear** with visible cracks, broken parts, copper oxidation (wax/scrape), vines, and a Garage that
  repairs part by part and charges by material.
- **Rules:** WHEN / DO / UNLESS rows, 8 rules, 23 triggers, 16 actions, 6 presets, chest A/B links, a post,
  the Golem Linker, harvesting and replanting, smelting, recharging, self repair, walking to the Garage, and
  the Golem Signal Plate.
- **Cubs:** 16 colors, sit, doze, greet, item magnet, 9 slots, grow into a golem after three parts.
- **Optional mods:** All The Ores metals, Allthemodium (plus its three alloys) and Silent Gear metals appear only
  when a mod provides the ingot tag.
